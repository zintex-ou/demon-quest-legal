Frequently asked

I bought something and it did not arrive

Make sure you are connected to the internet and signed in to Google Play with the same Google account you bought with, then restart the game. If it still has not arrived, email us your nickname and the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA.).

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are billed by Google Play, so refunds are handled by Google: open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or visit Google Play refund help.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play Games in the game. Your progress is saved to your Google Play Games account, and when you install the game on another device and sign in with the same account, it is restored. Without signing in, progress is stored only on your device and is lost if you delete the game.

The game asks me to update

Some updates are required to keep online features fair and working. Update the game from Google Play and your progress will stay.

The game says I am offline

Online features need an internet connection. Check your connection, switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, and make sure your phone’s date and time are set automatically.

Ads did not give me a reward

Rewards are given only after the video finishes. Sometimes no ad is available — try again a bit later. The number of rewarded ads per day may be limited.

How do I turn off personalised ads?

Use the ads personalisation switch in the game’s Settings. You can also manage your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Someone has an offensive nickname or is cheating in rankings

Email us the nickname and the ranking where you saw it. We review reports and remove entries that break our Terms of Use.

Delete my data

Demon Quest does not use accounts with an email or password. The data we hold about you on our servers is your nickname, its registration date, your ranking results and any uncollected gifts.

To delete it, email info@zintex.dev with the subject “Demon Quest data deletion” and your exact nickname. We delete your nickname, ranking entries and gifts within 30 days and confirm by email. Analytics data is deleted automatically after its retention period.

Progress saved on your phone is removed when you delete the game or clear its data. Progress saved in Google Play Games can be deleted in the Google Play Games app (Profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, or per game).
