Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Demon Quest : Dungeon RPG (“the game”, “we”) is published by Zintex OÜ, a company registered in Estonia (zintex.dev). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, who processes it and what choices you have. We do not sell your personal data.

What we use

Game progress. Your heroes, items, currencies, settings and progress are saved on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy of your save is also stored in your Google Play Games account so you can continue on another device.

Nickname and rankings. When you choose a nickname, it is registered together with the date it was created. If you take part in rankings, your nickname is stored with your ranking results (such as score, difficulty, tier, character level, clear time and the time of the record). Nicknames and ranking results are visible to other players. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as a nickname.

Gifts. Rewards we send to players (for example, compensation or event gifts) are stored under your nickname until you collect them in the game.

Technical identifiers. The game signs you in anonymously to our game server, which creates a random technical identifier that is not linked to your name or email address. We also use the app version, device model, operating system and similar technical information to run the game, check for required updates, fight cheating and fix errors.

Usage analytics. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used (for example, app opens, sign-ins and session data), together with an app-instance identifier and approximate location derived from your IP address.

Purchases. In-game items are bought through Google Play. We receive the product, the purchase status and a purchase token to deliver and verify what you bought — never your payment card details.

Advertising. The game shows optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. Ad partners may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads, to deliver, measure and — where you have consented — personalise ads. See “Your advertising choices” below.

Achievements. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your achievements are reported to your Google Play Games profile.

Server time. To prevent clock manipulation, the game requests the current time from a server hosted on Google infrastructure; this request reveals your IP address to that service, but nothing else is sent.

Service providers

Data is processed only as needed by:

  • Google — distribution and purchases through Google Play, Google Play Games (sign-in, cloud saves, achievements), Firebase (anonymous authentication, Realtime Database for nicknames, rankings and gifts, Analytics), Google AdMob (ads). Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Technologies — Unity LevelPlay ad mediation and ironSource Ads. Unity privacy policy
  • Adapty — verifying and managing in-app purchases. Adapty privacy policy

Each provider processes data according to its role and its own privacy commitments, and may process it outside the EU/EEA under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your advertising choices

You can turn personalised ads on or off at any time in the game’s Settings. If you turn them off, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised based on your activity. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Legal bases

Where EU/EEA or UK data-protection law applies: saving your progress, running rankings and gifts, and delivering and verifying purchases are necessary to provide the game you asked for; analytics, anti-cheat, security and fixing errors rely on our legitimate interest in a fair and reliable game; personalised advertising relies on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete the game or its data. Nicknames and ranking results are kept while the rankings exist, or until you ask us to delete them. Uncollected gifts are removed once collected or when no longer relevant. Analytics data is kept only for the retention period set in Google Analytics and is then deleted automatically. Purchase records are kept by Google and Adapty as long as needed to provide your purchases and meet legal obligations. Data in your Google Play Games account is controlled by you and Google.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the age of digital consent in their country without the required consent. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your personal data, and object to certain processing. To delete your nickname and ranking results, follow the steps on our data deletion section. You can also complain to a supervisory authority — in Estonia, the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon (aki.ee).

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be announced in the game.
